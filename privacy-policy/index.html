CalcKit Privacy Policy

Everyday calculations, made simple.

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CalcKit ("CalcKit", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the CalcKit mobile application.

CalcKit is an everyday calculator application that provides calculators for percentages, discounts, profit and loss, savings, fuel costs, electricity costs, dates, time, unit conversions, and other everyday calculations.

CalcKit is designed to perform its core calculations locally on your device. Your calculator inputs and calculation results are not sent to our servers for the purpose of performing calculations.

2. Information We Collect

CalcKit does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the core calculator features.

Depending on the version of the application and the services enabled, certain third-party services may collect limited information as described below.

Calculator Information

Information that you enter into calculators, such as prices, percentages, dates, distances, fuel efficiency, electricity consumption, or other calculation values, is processed locally on your device for the purpose of providing the requested result.

Favorites and History

If CalcKit provides favorites or calculation history features, this information may be stored locally on your device so that you can access it later.

3. Advertising

CalcKit may display advertisements using third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may automatically collect certain information from your device to provide, measure, and improve advertisements. This information may include device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, IP address, application information, and information about interactions with advertisements, depending on the applicable settings and consent requirements.

Advertising providers may use this information to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, depending on your settings, location, and applicable consent requirements.

You can learn more about how Google handles information for advertising through Google's privacy resources:

Google Privacy Policy

For more information about Google advertising technologies, please visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Analytics and Diagnostics

If analytics or crash-reporting services are enabled in a future version of CalcKit, these services may collect technical and usage information to help us understand application performance, identify crashes, and improve the application.

Such information may include application version, device type, operating system version, crash information, and general interaction information.

CalcKit does not use analytics services to intentionally collect the contents of your calculator inputs.

5. Third-Party Services

CalcKit may use third-party services to provide advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other application functionality.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples may include:

  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Google Play services
  • Analytics or crash-reporting services, if enabled

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of applicable third-party service providers.

6. Data Storage

CalcKit's core calculator functionality does not require your calculation data to be uploaded to a remote server.

Certain application features, such as favorites, recent calculators, settings, or calculation history, may store information locally on your device.

Removing the application may remove locally stored application data, depending on your device and operating system.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by CalcKit and the services used by the application.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

CalcKit is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the application.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through CalcKit, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have choices regarding personalized advertising and the processing of certain information by third-party advertising providers.

You may also control certain device-level privacy and advertising settings through your Android device and Google account.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to CalcKit, the services we use, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy periodically.
